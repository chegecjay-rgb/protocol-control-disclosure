## Preferred Canonical Terminology

### deterministic
Outputs are reproducible from equivalent replay inputs without probabilistic variance.

### canonical
A replay or representation normalized into a stable machine-checkable form.

### replay-safe
Replay execution that preserves deterministic equivalence guarantees.

### immutable
State or evidence that cannot be mutated without invalidating equivalence guarantees.

### bounded
Strictly constrained authority, semantics, and execution scope.

### stateless
Verification behavior that does not depend on hidden mutable runtime memory.

### machine-checkable
Capable of deterministic verification by formalized computational procedures.

### execution disclosure
Structured declaration of operational execution continuity.

### semantic declaration
Explicit declaration of execution meaning without governance arbitration.

### causality reconstruction
Deterministic reconstruction of replay relationships and execution lineage.

### deterministic consistency verification
Machine-checkable equivalence validation without governance interpretation.
